Open a Head and move funds, one step at a time. Everyone starts with
20 practice ADA. No wallet needed.

<HeadPlayground />

<Details summary="About this demo">

Drag from L1 into the same person's L2 row to deposit, or back to L1 to withdraw.
Drag between people on L2 to pay, or use **Send a payment**. Reserved funds stay
unavailable until their request completes. Use Back and Forward to replay steps.

Operators run nodes and sign snapshots. Wallet users connect through an operator
and keep their keys. L2 funds are backed by funds locked on L1.

The demo runs Hydra's Crem logic with SimpleTx. Each chip represents an output;
its owner and 10 ADA value are illustrations. L1, time, and signatures are
simulated. No real funds or keys are used.

Read the [protocol overview](./protocol-overview.md), try the
[real deposit flow](./how-to/incremental-commit.md), or explore
[how the browser runtime works](./dev/head-playground-runtime.mdx).

</Details>
