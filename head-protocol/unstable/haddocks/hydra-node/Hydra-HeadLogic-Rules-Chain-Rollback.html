On rollback, re-post the DecrementTx if there is a pending decommit whose confirmed snapshot contains a matching utxoToDecommit. The rollback may have erased the original on-chain DecrementTx observation.

If the decrement was already finalized (DecommitFinalized cleared StateChanged and $sel:confirmedSnapshot:CoordinatedHeadState may have advanced past the decrementing snapshot), fall back to the retained $sel:finalizedDecommit:CoordinatedHeadState — but only when the rollback reaches strictly before its observation slot. See #2741.

Chain rollback preserves L2 state and reposts work selected from the retained state. It does not reconstruct fanout progress on a divergent chain.
