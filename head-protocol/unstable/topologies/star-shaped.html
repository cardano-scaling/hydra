Star-shaped head network

Design proposal

This topology describes an application built from independent heads. Hydra provides the individual heads; it does not provide routing or atomic payments between them.

A service can participate in a separate two-party head with each client. It runs a hydra-node process for each head. Clients and the service must remain available to sign snapshots in their respective heads.

Funding and settlement​

Each head opens empty and receives its own deposits. The same L1 input cannot fund two heads: each deposit spends distinct inputs. Heads maintain separate UTxO sets, snapshot signatures, and settlement transactions.

A service needs liquidity in both heads and an application protocol to move economic value between them. An atomic transfer mechanism, its timeouts, and failure recovery must be designed separately. Copying a transaction or snapshot from one head to another does not provide these guarantees.

Off-chain coordination​

Each head uses its own snapshot leader rotation and requires signatures from all its parties. The service is not permanently the leader.

The illustrated sequence is not an atomic payment protocol. A complete design must explain what happens if either head stops progressing, closes, or loses its required liquidity between the two confirmations. Each head can close and settle independently; any coupling must be enforced by the application.
